FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 17

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about school subjects and student choice. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should teenagers be allowed to choose all of their own subjects at school, rather than having compulsory subjects?

Notes:
Write about:
1. studying things they are interested in
2. getting a broad education
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine for young people: Articles wanted: The Best Weekend Activity! Tell us what you enjoy doing most on a Saturday or Sunday. Do you prefer relaxing or being active? Why is it so enjoyable? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: When the lights suddenly went out in the school hall, nobody knew what was happening.

(c) Review: A new café or snack bar has recently opened near your school. Write a review for your school magazine. Describe the atmosphere and the food, say what you liked and disliked about it, and explain whether you would recommend it to your friends.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about the town or village where you live. What do you like about it?
  • How do you usually spend your free time during the week?
  • What are your plans for your education or career in the future?
  • What kind of music do you enjoy listening to, and why?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about studying in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to spend their free time in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Studying

Candidate B's pictures: Free time

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your local library be improved to attract more teenagers? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • providing free, high-speed Wi-Fi
  • opening a cafe with snacks and drinks
  • creating a dedicated gaming or media room
  • hosting regular talks by popular young adult authors
  • updating the furniture to be more comfortable

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think public libraries will still exist in fifty years' time? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that reading information on the internet is better than reading books. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for people to have a quiet place to go to in a busy town or city?
