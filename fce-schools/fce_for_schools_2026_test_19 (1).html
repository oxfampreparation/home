FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 19

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about rules for teenagers. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should teenagers have a strict curfew (a specific time they must be home) on weekends?

Notes:
Write about:
1. safety and avoiding trouble
2. socializing and independence
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine for young people: Articles wanted: Learning Languages! What do you think is the best way for teenagers to learn a new language? Is it better to study grammar in class or watch foreign movies? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: As Mark stepped onto the empty stage, he suddenly realised he had forgotten his lines.

(c) Review: A new fast-food restaurant or cafe has recently opened in your town and is very popular with teenagers. Write a review for an English-language teen blog. Describe the atmosphere and the food, say what you liked and disliked, and explain whether you would recommend it to your friends.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your hometown. What do you like most about living there?
  • How do you usually spend your free time on the weekends?
  • What are your plans for your education or career after you finish school?
  • Do you enjoy reading books for pleasure? Why/Why not?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about learning in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to relax in these different environments.

Candidate A's pictures: Learning

Candidate B's pictures: Relaxing

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a school encourage its students to read more books for pleasure? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • modernising the school library with new furniture
  • inviting famous authors to give talks at the school
  • providing digital e-readers or tablets for everyone
  • having thirty minutes of quiet reading time every day
  • starting student-run book clubs

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Some people say that reading information on a screen is not as good as reading a real paper book. Do you agree?
  • Do you think traditional printed books will completely disappear in the future? Why/Why not?
  • How important is it for parents to read to their children when they are young?
