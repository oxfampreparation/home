FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 9

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about school subjects and physical education. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should Physical Education (PE) be graded in the same way as academic subjects like maths and science?

Notes:
Write about:
1. measuring students' fitness levels
2. natural ability vs. hard work
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine for teenagers: Articles wanted: The Perfect Rainy Day! What is the best way to spend a weekend when the weather is terrible? Do you prefer to stay at home or find indoor activities to do with friends? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: When Mark opened his school locker, a mysterious blue envelope fell out onto the floor.

(c) Review: A new café or hangout spot has recently opened near your school. Write a review for your school magazine. Describe the atmosphere and the food, explain what you like and dislike about it, and say whether you would recommend it to other students.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • How much homework do you usually get each week? Do you think it's too much?
  • What do you enjoy doing in your free time on a Sunday afternoon?
  • Tell us about a piece of technology that you use every day.
  • What job would you like to have when you finish your education?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about reading in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say how you think the teenagers might be feeling about travelling to school in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Reading

Candidate B's pictures: Travelling to school

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your school cafeteria be improved to make it more popular with students? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • offering more vegetarian and vegan options
  • lowering the prices of hot meals
  • providing larger tables for groups to sit together
  • creating a faster queuing system
  • installing better vending machines for snacks

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think teenagers eat a healthier diet today than they did in the past? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that cooking should be a compulsory subject for all students at school. Do you agree?
  • How much do you think food advertising influences the choices that young people make?
