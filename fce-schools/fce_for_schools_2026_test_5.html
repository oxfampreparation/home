FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 5

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about school rules and technology. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should mobile phones be completely banned for students during the school day?

Notes:
Write about:
1. concentration in lessons
2. socializing during break times
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in your school's English magazine: Articles wanted: Summer Holidays! What is the best way for teenagers to spend their long summer holiday? Is it better to get a job, travel, or just relax? The most interesting articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: When Sarah opened the mysterious wooden box she had found in the school attic, she couldn't believe her eyes.

(c) Review: You recently went on a school trip to a science museum or art gallery. Write a review for an English-language student blog. Describe the exhibits, explain what you found most interesting or boring, and say whether you would recommend it to other schools.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What do you enjoy doing with your friends at the weekend?
  • Tell us about a hobby you'd like to try in the future.
  • Do you plan to go to university or get a job when you leave school?
  • How important is sport or exercise in your daily life?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about doing these different sports.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to learn in these different environments.

Candidate A's pictures: Doing sports

Candidate B's pictures: Learning environments

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your school become more environmentally friendly? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • putting recycling bins in every classroom
  • starting a school vegetable garden
  • turning off all lights and computers at the end of the day
  • banning single-use plastic bottles in the canteen
  • encouraging students to walk or cycle to school

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think young people today care more about the environment than older generations? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that individual actions (like recycling) don't make a big enough difference to climate change. Do you agree?
  • How do you think schools will change in the future to help protect the planet?
