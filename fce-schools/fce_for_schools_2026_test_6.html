FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 6

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about school food. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should secondary schools completely ban the sale of fast food and sugary snacks in the school canteen?

Notes:
Write about:
1. student health and energy levels
2. personal freedom and choice
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school magazine. Your story must begin with this sentence: As soon as the headteacher announced the winner of the competition, Maya couldn't believe her ears.

(b) Article: You see this announcement in a magazine for teenagers: Articles wanted: The Perfect Weekend Job! What do you think is the best kind of part-time job for a student? What can you learn from it, and how do you balance it with schoolwork? The best articles will be published next month.

(c) Review: You recently watched a movie or a television series that was set in a high school. Write a review for an English-language entertainment website for teenagers. Describe the main characters, explain whether you thought it was a realistic portrayal of school life, and say if you would recommend it to your friends.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What subjects are you currently studying at school, and which do you prefer?
  • Tell us about something you enjoy doing with your friends at the weekend.
  • Do you think you will stay in your hometown in the future, or move somewhere else?
  • How do you usually listen to music?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about learning in these different situations.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to spend their free time in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Learning

Candidate B's pictures: Spending free time

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your school improve its sports and fitness facilities to encourage more students to participate? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • buying new, modern gym equipment
  • starting an after-school dance club
  • building a new outdoor running track
  • offering a wider variety of team sports
  • allowing students to choose their own PE activities

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think physical education is just as important as academic subjects like maths and science? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that teenage life is much more stressful today than it was in the past. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for teenagers to have a hobby that doesn't involve looking at a screen?
