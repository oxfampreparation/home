FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 14

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about school canteens and student health. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should secondary schools completely ban all unhealthy food and sugary drinks from the school canteen?

Notes:
Write about:
1. student health and concentration
2. personal choice and freedom
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine for young people: Articles wanted: The Perfect Summer Job! What is the best kind of part-time job for a teenager during the long summer holiday? What can you learn from it, and how does it help prepare you for the future? The most interesting articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: As soon as the headteacher announced the winner of the competition, Maya couldn't believe her ears.

(c) Review: You recently watched a movie or a television series that was set in a high school. Write a review for an English-language entertainment website for teenagers. Describe the main characters, explain whether you thought it was a realistic portrayal of school life, and say if you would recommend it to your friends.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your family. Do you spend a lot of time with them?
  • What is your favourite subject at school? Why do you enjoy it?
  • Do you prefer to spend your holidays in the countryside or in a big city?
  • What kind of career do you hope to have in the future?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about doing these sports.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to study in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Doing sports

Candidate B's pictures: Studying

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could a town improve its public park to make it more popular with young people? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • building a new outdoor skatepark
  • providing free, high-speed Wi-Fi
  • opening a modern cafe with cheap snacks
  • organising regular outdoor music concerts
  • creating an open-air cinema screen

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think it is important for every town to have free public spaces? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say teenagers spend too much time indoors. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for local councils to listen to the opinions of young people?
