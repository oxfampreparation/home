FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 15

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about chores and responsibility. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should all teenagers be expected to do household chores (like cleaning and cooking) for their family?

Notes:
Write about:
1. learning responsibility for the future
2. having enough time to study
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in a school magazine: Articles wanted: The Best Way to Relax! Tell us how you prefer to relax after a long, stressful week at school. Do you play sports, watch movies, or hang out with friends? Why is your way the best? The most interesting articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for a local writing competition. Your story must begin with this sentence: When the school bus finally arrived, nobody was prepared for what happened next.

(c) Review: A new clothes shop aimed at teenagers has recently opened in your town. Write a review for an English-language teen blog. Describe the clothes they sell, comment on the prices and the atmosphere, and say whether you would recommend it to your friends.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your home. What do you like most about it?
  • How do you usually spend your free time during the week?
  • What are your plans for your future education or career?
  • Do you enjoy reading books for pleasure? Why/Why not?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about helping other people in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to exercise in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Helping others

Candidate B's pictures: Exercising

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could a school improve its annual sports day to make it more popular with all students? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • allowing students to choose the sports they play
  • inviting famous local athletes to give out prizes
  • organising fun, non-competitive games
  • providing better snacks and drinks on the day
  • having a music concert at the end of the event

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think it is important for everyone to participate in sports, even if they aren't very good at them? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that schools focus too much on winning competitions rather than just having fun. Do you agree?
  • How important is it to be a good loser in sports and in life?
