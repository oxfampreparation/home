FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 1

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about homework. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it better for students to get a little bit of homework every day, or a large amount of homework to do over the weekend?

Notes:
Write about:
1. having free time to relax
2. remembering what you studied in class
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in your school's English magazine: Articles wanted: My Favourite Teacher! Think about a teacher who really inspired you. What subject did they teach? Why did you enjoy their classes so much? The best articles will be published next month!

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: As soon as the final school bell rang, Tom grabbed his bag and ran out the door.

(c) Email: You have received an email from your English friend, Alex. "I'm joining your school as an exchange student next month! I'm really nervous because I don't know anyone. What is the school like? What kind of clubs or sports can I join to make new friends?" Write an email responding to Alex.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your favourite subject at school, and why?
  • Tell us about something you enjoy doing at the weekend.
  • Do you prefer to study on your own or with friends? Why?
  • What kind of job would you like to have in the future?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the students have chosen to study in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about these different types of school trips.

Candidate A's pictures: Studying

Candidate B's pictures: School Trips

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a school help its students be more environmentally friendly? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the easiest to achieve.

  • putting recycling bins in every classroom
  • starting a school vegetable garden
  • encouraging students to walk or cycle to school
  • using tablets to reduce the amount of paper used
  • making sure all lights are switched off after class

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think young people worry more about the environment than older people do? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that schools should teach 'environmental studies' as a compulsory subject. Do you agree?
  • How can teenagers convince their parents to be greener at home?
