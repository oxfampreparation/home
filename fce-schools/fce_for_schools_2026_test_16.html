FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 16

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about teenagers and work. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should teenagers be allowed to have part-time jobs during the school term?

Notes:
Write about:
1. managing time and schoolwork
2. earning their own money
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement on an English-language student website: Articles wanted: The Best School Subject! Tell us about the subject you enjoy studying the most. Why is it so interesting, and how do you think it will help you in the future? The most inspiring articles will be published next week.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school magazine. Your story must begin with this sentence: When the school bell rang, Tom realized he was completely alone in the building.

(c) Review: You recently watched a new streaming series that is very popular with teenagers. Write a review for an English-language teen blog. Describe the story, explain what you liked or disliked about the actors, and say whether you would recommend it to your friends.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about the town or city where you live.
  • What do you usually like to do in your free time?
  • What are your plans for your education or career in the future?
  • Do you enjoy reading books for pleasure? Why/Why not?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are finding difficult about learning these new skills.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say how you think the teenagers might be feeling about travelling to school in these different conditions.

Candidate A's pictures: Learning a new skill

Candidate B's pictures: Travelling to school

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a school encourage its students to read more books for pleasure? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • modernising the school library space
  • providing digital e-readers or tablets
  • inviting famous authors to give talks
  • having thirty minutes of quiet reading time every day
  • starting student book clubs

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think teenagers read fewer books today than they did in the past? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that reading information on a screen is not as good as reading a real paper book. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for parents to read to their children when they are young?
