FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 3

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about school curriculums and future careers. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should secondary schools focus more on teaching practical life skills, such as cooking and managing money, rather than traditional academic subjects?

Notes:
Write about:
1. preparing for independent adult life
2. getting a place at a university
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine for young people: Articles wanted: My Favourite Gadget! Tell us about a piece of technology you use every day. Why is it so important to you, and how would your life be different without it? The most interesting articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school's creative writing magazine. Your story must begin with this sentence: The moment the train doors closed, Sarah realised she had left her bag on the platform.

(c) Review: You recently watched a movie that features teenagers as the main characters. Write a review for an English-language student website. Describe the story, say whether you think it gives a realistic picture of teenage life, and explain if you would recommend it to your friends.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about the area where you live. What do you like most about it?
  • What do you usually do to relax after a long day at school?
  • Which school subject do you find the most difficult, and why?
  • Do you have any exciting plans for the next school holidays?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about doing these creative activities.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to exercise in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Creative activities

Candidate B's pictures: Exercising

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could a school improve its canteen to encourage students to eat there more often? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • lowering the prices of hot meals
  • providing more comfortable seating areas
  • offering a wider choice of healthy snacks
  • playing background music during lunch breaks
  • serving food from different countries each week

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think schools should be allowed to ban students from bringing unhealthy food in their lunchboxes? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say teenagers don't know enough about cooking healthy meals. Do you agree?
  • How much do you think advertising influences the food choices that young people make?
