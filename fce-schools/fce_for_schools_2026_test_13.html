FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 13

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about teenagers and money. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should teenagers be given a weekly allowance (pocket money) by their parents without having to do any household chores?

Notes:
Write about:
1. learning the value of hard work
2. having enough time for school studies
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in your school's English magazine: Articles wanted: The Best Way to Spend a Saturday! Tell us what your ideal Saturday looks like. Do you prefer staying indoors or going outside? Who do you spend it with? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: When I opened my locker on Monday morning, a strange old map fell out onto the floor.

(c) Review: Your class recently went on a school trip to a historical site or museum. Write a review of the trip for your college magazine. Describe what you saw there, explain what was most interesting about it, and say whether you think future classes should go there too.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your hometown. What is the most popular place for teenagers to hang out?
  • What do you usually do in your free time when you want to relax?
  • What are your plans for your education or career in the future?
  • Do you enjoy reading books for pleasure? Why/Why not?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to eat their lunch in these different situations.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about travelling during these different seasons.

Candidate A's pictures: Eating lunch

Candidate B's pictures: Travelling in different seasons

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your school become more environmentally friendly? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • putting recycling bins in every classroom
  • starting a school vegetable garden
  • banning single-use plastic bottles in the canteen
  • organising a 'walk or cycle to school' week
  • turning off all lights and computers at the end of the day

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think young people care more about the environment than older generations do? Why?
  • Some people say that individual actions (like recycling) don't make a big difference, and it is entirely the government's responsibility. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for schools to teach students about climate change?
