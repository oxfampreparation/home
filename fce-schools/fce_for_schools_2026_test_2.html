FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 2

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about video games and free time. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should parents limit the amount of time teenagers spend playing video games?

Notes:
Write about:
1. doing homework and getting enough sleep
2. socialising and playing with friends online
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school's English magazine. Your story must begin with this sentence: As the teacher opened the classroom door, everyone gasped.

(b) Article: You see this announcement on an English-language website for teenagers: Articles wanted: My Favourite Weekend Activity! Tell us about how you like to spend your weekends. What do you do, and why is it so much fun? We will publish the most interesting articles next month.

(c) Review: You recently watched a movie that is very popular with teenagers right now. Write a review for an English-language entertainment blog. Describe the story, explain why you think teenagers like it so much, and say whether you would recommend it.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What subjects do you enjoy most at school? Why?
  • Tell us about what you usually do after school finishes.
  • Do you have any plans for what you want to do when you leave school?
  • How important is it for you to keep up to date with the news?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are finding difficult about studying in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to exercise in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Studying

Candidate B's pictures: Exercising

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could a school improve its canteen to make it more popular with students? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the best to introduce.

  • providing more vegetarian and vegan options
  • allowing students to have a longer lunch break
  • lowering the prices of hot meals
  • installing vending machines for quick snacks
  • playing music in the background

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think it is important for teenagers to eat healthy food at school? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that students should learn how to cook basic meals at school. Do you agree?
  • How much influence do you think advertising has on the food teenagers choose to buy?
