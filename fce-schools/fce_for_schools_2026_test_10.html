FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 10

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about friendship. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it better to have a few very close friends or a large group of casual friends?

Notes:
Write about:
1. trusting people with secrets
2. having different people for different activities
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an international magazine for teenagers: Articles wanted: The Importance of Sleep! Do teenagers get enough sleep these days? How does a lack of sleep affect school performance and mood? Tell us your thoughts and tips for getting a good night's rest. The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: As the school bus finally arrived at the campsite, everyone stared out of the window in shock.

(c) Review: You recently read an interesting book about a famous historical figure or event. Write a review for your school magazine. Describe what the book was about, explain what you liked or disliked about it, and say whether you would recommend it to other students.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your family. Do you have any brothers or sisters?
  • What is your favourite room in your house, and why?
  • Tell us about a hobby you have recently started or would like to start.
  • Do you prefer to spend your free time indoors or outdoors?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are finding difficult about learning in these different situations.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to spend time with animals in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Learning

Candidate B's pictures: Time with animals

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your town provide better facilities for young people? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most popular with teenagers.

  • building a new indoor skatepark
  • opening a modern cinema complex
  • creating a free outdoor sports area
  • providing a safe youth club for the evenings
  • organising regular live music events

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think teenagers today spend too much time indoors? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that the government should pay for all public sports facilities. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for young people to have spaces where they can meet without adults present?
