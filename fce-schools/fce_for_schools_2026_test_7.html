FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 7

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about money and teenagers. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should teenagers be given a regular weekly allowance (pocket money) by their parents, or should they have to earn it by doing household chores?

Notes:
Write about:
1. learning the value of money
2. having enough time for schoolwork
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in your school magazine: Articles wanted: The Best School Trip! Tell us about an amazing school trip you went on. Where did you go, what did you learn, and why was it so much fun? The best articles will be published in our next issue.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: As soon as the teacher announced the groups for the science project, Mark felt a sudden wave of panic.

(c) Review: You recently watched a popular new movie that everyone at school is talking about. Write a review for an English-language teen magazine. Describe the story, explain what you liked or disliked about the actors, and say whether you think teenagers will enjoy it.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Where is your favourite place to go in your hometown?
  • Tell us about a hobby you do at the weekend.
  • What job would you like to have when you finish your education?
  • What kind of music do you enjoy listening to, and why?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about taking a break from their studies in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to communicate in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Taking a break

Candidate B's pictures: Communicating

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your school improve its canteen/cafeteria to make it more popular with students? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • lowering the prices of hot meals
  • offering more vegetarian and healthy options
  • installing more comfortable seating
  • allowing students to play their own music
  • extending the opening hours during breaks

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think it is important for teenagers to learn how to cook at school? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that traditional family dinners are becoming less common. Do you agree?
  • How much do you think advertising influences the food that young people choose to buy?
