FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 4

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about free time and hobbies. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it better for teenagers to spend their free time playing team sports or doing creative activities like art and music?

Notes:
Write about:
1. physical health and fitness
2. expressing your feelings and ideas
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in your school's English magazine: Articles wanted: The Best School Subject! Tell us about your favourite subject at school. Why is it so interesting, and how will it be useful to you in the future? The most interesting articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for a competition. Your story must begin with this sentence: When the school bell finally rang, Tom realised he had lost the most important thing in his bag.

(c) Review: You recently played a new video game or mobile game that is very popular right now. Write a review for an English-language website for young people. Describe what the game is about, explain what you like and dislike about the graphics or gameplay, and say if you would recommend it to your friends.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about the town or village where you live.
  • What is your favourite subject at school, and why?
  • How do you usually spend your time during the school holidays?
  • What kind of career would you like to have in the future?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say how you think the teenagers feel about helping out in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about travelling to school in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Helping at home

Candidate B's pictures: Travelling to school

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your school improve the canteen to make it more popular with students? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • lowering the prices of the hot meals
  • providing a wider choice of vegetarian food
  • allowing students to listen to music during lunch
  • installing more comfortable seating areas
  • having themed food days from different countries

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think it's important for students to eat a healthy meal at lunchtime? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say teenagers should learn how to cook basic meals at school. Do you agree?
  • How much do you think advertising influences the snacks that young people buy?
