FCE for Schools 2026 - Test 12

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about technology in education. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should teenagers be allowed to use mobile phones in class for educational purposes?

Notes:
Write about:
1. finding information quickly
2. distraction from learning
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in your school's English magazine: Articles wanted: The Perfect Weekend Activity! What do you think is the best way for teenagers to spend a free weekend? Why is it so enjoyable, and who do you usually do it with? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Story: Your English teacher has asked you to write a story for the school website. Your story must begin with this sentence: When the school alarm suddenly rang, nobody knew what was happening.

(c) Review: You recently downloaded a new educational app that helps students learn a subject like maths or a foreign language. Write a review for a teenage technology blog. Describe how the app works, explain what you like or dislike about it, and say whether you would recommend it to other students.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your hometown. What is the most interesting part of it?
  • What do you enjoy doing in your free time when the weather is bad?
  • Tell us about a hobby you've had for a long time.
  • What kind of job or studies do you hope to pursue in the future?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the teenagers are enjoying about studying in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the teenagers have chosen to exercise in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Studying

Candidate B's pictures: Exercising

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your school library be improved to make it more popular with students? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • allowing students to bring in drinks and snacks
  • buying more modern laptops and tablets
  • hosting regular talks by famous authors
  • providing more comfortable seating areas
  • extending the opening hours after school

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think teenagers today read fewer books than in the past? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that traditional paper books will eventually disappear completely. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for schools to use the latest technology in every lesson?
