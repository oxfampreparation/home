Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more.

Your friend Max wants to come to the town music day. Write an email. Tell Max where to meet, which performance you want to hear, and what to bring.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more about a student who learns to play a new instrument.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

These are original B1 practice scripts read aloud by your browser’s text-to-speech voice. Each recording can be played twice. This is not official Cambridge audio.

Speaking (8–10 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

  • What kind of music do you like?
  • Do you play an instrument?
  • When do you usually listen to music?
  • Have you ever been to a concert?
  • What do you enjoy doing with friends?

Part 2 — Discussion

Talk with your partner about activities at a town music day. Which would you like to do? Give reasons and ask your partner.

  • sing in a group
  • try a new instrument
  • watch a band
  • learn a dance
