Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more.

Your friend Olivia wants to come on your school camp. Write an email. Tell Olivia where you are going, what to pack, and which activity you are looking forward to.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more. Use the three pictures to tell a story about friends who get lost on a walk and find their way back.

0 words

Listening (about 30 minutes)

These are original B1 practice scripts read aloud by your browser’s text-to-speech voice. Each recording can be played twice. This is not official Cambridge audio.

Speaking (8–10 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

  • Do you enjoy spending time outdoors?
  • Have you ever stayed in a tent?
  • What do you like doing on a weekend?
  • Where do you go for a walk?
  • What would you take on a day trip?

Part 2 — Discussion

Talk with your partner about activities on an outdoor school trip. Which would you like to try? Give reasons and ask your partner.

  • follow a nature trail
  • build a small shelter
  • watch birds
  • cook outdoors
  • play a team game
