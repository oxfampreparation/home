Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more.

Your friend Alex wants to visit the railway museum. Write an email. Tell Alex what day to go, where to meet, and what you want to see.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more. Use the pictures to tell a story about a child who finds a lost ticket and catches the train.

0 words

Listening (about 30 minutes)

These are original B1 practice scripts read aloud by your browser’s text-to-speech voice. Each recording can be played twice. This is not official Cambridge audio.

Speaking (8–10 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

  • Do you enjoy travelling by train?
  • Where do you like going with your family?
  • What do you usually take on a day trip?
  • Have you visited a museum?
  • What would you like to see in another town?

Part 2 — Discussion

Talk with your partner about activities on a day out at a railway museum. Which would you like to do? Give reasons and ask your partner.

  • ride on an old train
  • look at railway maps
  • watch a short film
  • draw a train
  • have lunch in the café
