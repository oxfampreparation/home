Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more.

Your friend Mia wants to learn to cook. Write an email. Tell Mia what you can cook, when you can meet, and what ingredients to bring.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more about friends preparing a meal for a visitor.

Speaking (8–10 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

  • What food do you enjoy?
  • Can you cook anything?
  • Who do you eat dinner with?
  • What do you like doing at weekends?
  • Do you prefer eating at home or in a café?

Part 2 — Discussion

Talk with your partner about food for a school festival. Which would you like to prepare? Give reasons and ask your partner.
