Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email or note (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more. Include all the information in the notes.

Your English friend Alex is visiting you on Saturday. Write an email to Alex.

Tell Alex:
• what time to come
• what you can do together
• what to bring.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more. Use the three pictures to tell a story.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

These are original B1 practice scripts read aloud by your browser’s text-to-speech voice. Each recording can be played twice. This is not official Cambridge audio.

Speaking (8–10 minutes per pair)

Practise with a partner. The test has two parts: an interview and a discussion. Ask questions, answer clearly and give simple reasons.

Part 1 — Interview (3–4 minutes)

Answer questions about yourself and your everyday life.

  • Where do you live?
  • What do you like doing at the weekend?
  • What is your favourite food?
  • How do you usually travel to school?
  • Who do you spend your free time with?
  • What would you like to learn to do?

Part 2 — Discussion (5–6 minutes)

You and your partner are talking about places you can visit at the weekend. Look at the pictures. Ask and answer questions, say what you like or do not like, and give reasons.

Ideas: Which place would you like to visit? What can you do there? Would you rather go with friends or family? Why?
