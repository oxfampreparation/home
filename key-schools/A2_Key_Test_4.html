Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more.

Your friend Alex wants to join the drama club. Write an email. Tell Alex when the club meets, what job you would like to do, and what Alex should bring.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more. Tell a story about friends preparing costumes for their school play.

0 words

Listening (about 30 minutes)

These are original B1 practice scripts read aloud by your browser’s text-to-speech voice. Each recording can be played twice. This is not official Cambridge audio.

Speaking — 8–10 minutes per pair

Part 1 — Interview

  • What do you enjoy doing after school?
  • Do you like music or acting?
  • Have you ever been to a play?
  • What do you do with your friends at weekends?
  • What would you like to learn?

Part 2 — Discussion

Talk with your partner about jobs for a school show. Which would you like to do? Give reasons and ask your partner what they think.

  • acting
  • making costumes
  • painting scenery
  • playing music
  • welcoming visitors
