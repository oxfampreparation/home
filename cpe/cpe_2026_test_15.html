Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (1 hour 30 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (1 hour 30 minutes)

Part 1 - Discursive Essay (compulsory)

Read the two texts below. Write an essay summarising and evaluating the key points from both texts. Use your own words throughout as far as possible, and include your own ideas in your answers.

Text 1: The Necessity of Universal Basic Income
As artificial intelligence and rapid automation ruthlessly systematically dismantle the traditional labor market, the implementation of a Universal Basic Income (UBI) is no longer a utopian fantasy; it is an absolute structural necessity. Providing every citizen with a guaranteed, unconditional financial safety net is the only viable mechanism to prevent catastrophic societal fracturing and extreme wealth concentration. Critics who claim UBI will disincentivize labor fundamentally misunderstand human psychology. Relieved from the agonizing, paralyzing stress of basic survival, individuals are liberated to pursue meaningful, socially beneficial endeavours—such as higher education, creative arts, or community care—that the ruthless free market historically refuses to compensate.

Text 2: The Dignity of Guaranteed Employment
While the specter of automation looms large, addressing economic precarity through a Universal Basic Income is a philosophically hollow, dangerous panacea. Simply handing out cash ignores the profound psychological reality that human beings derive immense self-worth, structure, and social integration from structured employment. Instead of settling for a permanent class of subsidized, idle citizens, governments must enact a Federal Job Guarantee. The state must step in as the employer of last resort, aggressively funding massive public works programs to rebuild crumbling infrastructure and transition to green energy. This ensures that every citizen is not just kept alive, but is actively integrated into the vital civic mission of their nation.

Write your essay in 240 - 280 words.

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 280 - 320 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Report: Your multinational corporation is suffering from severe employee burnout. The HR director has asked you to write a report on the feasibility of implementing a permanent transition to asynchronous, flexible working hours. You should evaluate the potential impact on overall corporate productivity, analyze the likely effects on team cohesion and communication, and provide a clear recommendation on how to implement a secure pilot program.

(b) Review: A prominent digital media outlet is running a special feature on the cultural obsession with true crime. They are seeking reviews of highly successful investigative podcasts. Write a review of a podcast you have listened to, evaluating how effectively the creator balances compelling narrative tension with profound respect for the victims, and analyzing what the popularity of the genre reveals about modern psychological anxieties.

(c) Letter: Your local municipal council has proposed the complete demolition of a deeply historic, though currently derelict, Victorian factory complex to make way for a massive, heavily modern glass shopping centre. Write a letter to the editor of your local newspaper fiercely opposing this plan. You should systematically dismantle the council's economic arguments, highlighting the intrinsic value of preserving architectural heritage and proposing alternative, adaptive reuse strategies for the historic site.

Speaking (16 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test involves two candidates and two examiners.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • In what ways do you believe our reliance on GPS navigation alters our perception of our local environment?
  • How important is it for generations to actively document their family histories?
  • To what extent do you feel that extreme wealth invariably isolates an individual from society?
  • If you could permanently eliminate one aspect of modern digital communication, what would it be?

Part 2 - Collaborative Task (4 minutes)

Here are two photographs illustrating profoundly different approaches to manufacturing and labor. First, talk together about what specific cognitive and emotional demands these contrasting environments place on the workers. Then, decide which environment is more vulnerable to sudden, catastrophic disruption.

Part 3 - Long Turn & Discussion (10 minutes)

Candidate A (2 minutes): Look at the prompt card and talk about it. Candidate B will then answer a question.

To what extent does the modern obsession with 'wellness' mask a deeper societal sickness?
  • The extreme commercialization and commodification of health
  • The shifting of blame for stress from toxic work cultures to the individual
  • The profound psychological pressure to constantly 'optimize' oneself

Question for Candidate B: Do you believe alternative medicine should be strictly regulated by government medical bodies?

Candidate B (2 minutes): Look at the prompt card and talk about it. Candidate A will then answer a question.

How is the phenomenon of the 'gig economy' fundamentally altering the concept of a career?
  • The illusion of autonomy versus the reality of absolute precarity
  • The systematic dismantling of traditional worker protections and pensions
  • The psychological impact of lacking long-term professional stability

Question for Candidate A: Is the traditional nine-to-five office structure now entirely an obsolete relic?

General Discussion:

  • Some cultural critics argue that true rebellion in the 21st century involves completely disconnecting from the internet. What is your assessment of this?
  • How might the widespread deployment of autonomous surveillance drones permanently alter the concept of civic freedom?
  • Does the intense global focus on STEM subjects (Science, Tech, Engineering, Math) dangerously devalue the study of human history and philosophy?
