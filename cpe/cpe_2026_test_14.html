Writing (1 hour 30 minutes)

Part 1 - Discursive Essay (compulsory)

Read the two texts below. Write an essay summarising and evaluating the key points from both texts. Use your own words throughout as far as possible, and include your own ideas in your answers.

Text 1: The Utilitarian University
As global economic pressures intensify, the traditional paradigm of the university as an ivory tower dedicated to the pure, unadulterated pursuit of knowledge is becoming an unsustainable luxury. Taxpayers and students alike rightly demand a tangible return on their astronomical financial investments. Consequently, higher education must aggressively pivot towards a strictly utilitarian model, prioritizing the expansion of STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) faculties and vocational training. Defunding abstract humanities programs, which frequently fail to equip graduates with highly marketable skills, is a necessary structural adjustment. Universities must function primarily as efficient pipelines, directly supplying the corporate sector with a highly specialized, technically proficient workforce ready to drive national GDP.

Text 2: The Intrinsic Value of Inquiry
The accelerating commodification of higher education represents a profound cultural tragedy. By transforming universities into mere vocational training centers, we actively destroy the foundational pillars of critical civic discourse. The humanities—philosophy, history, literature—do not exist merely to generate immediate economic capital; they exist to cultivate the rigorous analytical frameworks required to interrogate power, understand complex historical traumas, and navigate deep ethical ambiguities. A society populated solely by highly skilled technicians, devoid of the philosophical capacity to question the ultimate purpose of their technological creations, is inherently vulnerable to authoritarianism and moral decay. The university must remain a sacred space for intellectual exploration, fiercely protected from the ruthless demands of short-term corporate logic.

Write your essay in 240 - 280 words.

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 280 - 320 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Report: The board of directors at your regional hospital has commissioned you to write a report on the feasibility of integrating AI-driven diagnostic tools into the emergency department. You should evaluate the potential improvements in patient triage efficiency, analyze the significant ethical concerns regarding algorithmic bias and medical liability, and provide a clear recommendation on how to implement a cautious, hybrid pilot program.

(b) Review: A leading cultural magazine is publishing a special issue on 'Cinema that Challenges our Humanity'. They are seeking reviews of science fiction films that successfully grapple with profound bioethical dilemmas. Write a review describing a film you have watched, evaluating how effectively the director balances spectacular visual effects with deep philosophical inquiry, and analyzing whether the film's ultimate message is one of technological optimism or profound existential dread.

(c) Letter: Your local municipal council has proposed a highly controversial zoning law that would permit the construction of massive, heavily automated distribution warehouses in a designated greenbelt area, citing significant job creation. Write a letter to the editor of your local newspaper fiercely opposing this legislation. You should systematically dismantle the council's economic arguments, highlighting the long-term ecological devastation, the increase in heavy freight traffic, and the erosion of community well-being.

Speaking (16 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test involves two candidates and two examiners.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • In what ways do you believe your consumption of news media has changed over the last decade?
  • How important is it for individuals to actively study the history of other nations, not just their own?
  • To what extent do you feel that mastering a second language alters a person's underlying personality?
  • If you were forced to abandon all modern digital devices for a month, what aspect of daily life would you find most challenging?

Part 2 - Collaborative Task (4 minutes)

Here are two photographs illustrating vastly different professional environments. First, talk together about what specific cognitive and emotional demands these contrasting environments place on the people working within them. Then, decide which environment is more likely to foster genuine, long-lasting collaborative innovation.

Part 3 - Long Turn & Discussion (10 minutes)

Candidate A (2 minutes): Look at the prompt card and talk about it. Candidate B will then answer a question.

To what extent will global demographic collapse reshape modern society?
  • The impending crisis of funding state pensions and healthcare
  • The necessity of drastically increasing automated labour
  • The psychological impact of aging populations on cultural innovation

Question for Candidate B: Do you believe governments should implement aggressive financial incentives to encourage higher birth rates?

Candidate B (2 minutes): Look at the prompt card and talk about it. Candidate A will then answer a question.

How is the algorithmic curation of information affecting the integrity of traditional journalism?
  • The prioritization of sensationalism over nuanced investigation
  • The economic collapse of localized, independent media outlets
  • The echo-chamber effect and its impact on civic discourse

Question for Candidate A: Is the concept of true journalistic objectivity a historical myth that we need to abandon?

General Discussion:

  • Some sociologists argue that the concept of 'privacy' is a modern invention that will inevitably disappear. What is your assessment of this?
  • How might the widespread adoption of universal basic income fundamentally alter the nature of human ambition?
  • Does the aggressive pursuit of economic globalization inevitably lead to the tragic erasure of unique indigenous cultures?
