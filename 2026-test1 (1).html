CAE 2026 - Test 1

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a panel discussion on how technology affects education. You have made notes on three points: online learning, AI tools, social media in the classroom. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the points. Say which is more important and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Article: a magazine invites readers to write about a place in their country that every visitor should see. (b) Letter: you stayed at a hotel and had problems; write to the manager. (c) Review: a film that changed the way you think. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Where are you from, and what do you like about living there?
  • What do you do in your free time?
  • Tell us about a skill you would like to learn.
  • How important is travelling to you?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say why the people might have chosen to study in these ways. Then answer: which way of studying do you think is more effective?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

What could schools do to prepare students for the world of work? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO are most useful.

  • work experience placements
  • learning practical skills
  • visits from local employers
  • group projects
  • career advice sessions

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Some people say young people today have too many choices. What do you think?
  • How far is success at work a matter of luck?
  • Do you think people should change careers during their lives?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
