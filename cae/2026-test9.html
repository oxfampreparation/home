CAE 2026 - Test 9

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on the influence of the media on society. You have made notes on three points: social media and personal privacy, advertising aimed at young people, news presented as entertainment. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the points. Say which is the greater concern and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Article: an international magazine is running a series called 'A skill worth learning'; write an article about a skill you believe everyone should acquire and explain why. (b) Letter: the mayor of your town has asked residents for their views on a plan to replace a historic building with a shopping centre; write a letter giving your opinion. (c) Report: your college wants to improve its facilities for students who study independently; write a report for the principal assessing the current facilities and recommending improvements. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your daily routine like on a typical working or study day?
  • Tell us about a person who has had a big influence on you.
  • Do you prefer to spend your free time indoors or outdoors? Why?
  • What skill would you most like to learn in the next few years?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of shopping in these ways might be. Then answer: which way do you think people will prefer in the future, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can schools best prepare young people for working life? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most important.

  • work experience placements
  • teaching practical skills such as budgeting
  • learning foreign languages
  • using up-to-date technology
  • group projects and teamwork

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Is formal education more important than life experience?
  • Should young people take a year out before starting university or a career?
  • How might education change over the next twenty years?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
