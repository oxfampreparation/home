CAE 2026 - Test 13

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on food and health. You have made notes on three points: the advertising of unhealthy food, school meals, the cost of fresh food. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the points. Say which is more important and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Proposal: your college wants to organise a cultural exchange week; write a proposal suggesting activities and explaining how they would benefit students. (b) Article: an English-language magazine has invited readers to describe a festival or tradition from their country; write an article saying what visitors could learn from it. (c) Report: your company wants to reduce its environmental impact; write a report assessing current practices and recommending changes. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • How did you spend last weekend?
  • Tell us about a subject you enjoyed studying at school.
  • Do you prefer to plan things in advance, or to be spontaneous?
  • What would you like to be doing in five years' time?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of living in these places might be. Then answer: which would suit you better at different stages of your life, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can people be encouraged to protect the environment? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • recycling schemes
  • higher taxes on polluting vehicles
  • environmental education in schools
  • cheaper public transport
  • support for local products

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do individuals or governments have more responsibility for protecting the environment?
  • Is it fair to expect developing countries to make the same changes as rich ones?
  • How will our daily lives be different in thirty years because of environmental concerns?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
