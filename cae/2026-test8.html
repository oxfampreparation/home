CAE 2026 - Test 8

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on ways of reducing food waste. You have made notes on three ideas: better storage at home, changes to supermarket policies, public awareness campaigns. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the ideas. Say which is more effective and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Proposal: your local sports club wants to attract more members; write a proposal. (b) Article: a magazine for young people wants articles on a skill everyone should learn; write your article. (c) Letter: write to the editor of a newspaper in reply to an article about banning mobile phones in schools. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Where would you most like to travel, and why?
  • What is your daily routine like?
  • What are you most proud of?
  • Tell us about a teacher you remember well.

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say why people might choose to spend an evening in these ways. Then answer: which way of spending an evening would you prefer, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can schools help students to develop good study habits? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • lessons in study skills
  • quiet study areas
  • less homework
  • regular feedback from teachers
  • group projects

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Is it better to specialise or to learn a little about everything?
  • How much should students be tested?
  • Will technology eventually replace teachers?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
