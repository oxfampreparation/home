CAE 2026 - Test 18

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on conserving water. You have made notes on three points: household habits, water use in industry and farming, government regulation. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the points. Say which would be more effective and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Report: the principal of your college wants to improve the library; write a report assessing the current service and recommending changes. (b) Review: write a review of a documentary or television series that you would recommend, explaining what viewers can learn from it. (c) Letter: a local newspaper has reported a plan to build a car park on a playing field; write a letter giving your views. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What kind of music do you enjoy listening to?
  • Tell us about a time when you felt nervous.
  • How do you usually prepare for something important?
  • What would you do if you had a completely free day tomorrow?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of attending music events like these might be. Then answer: which would you prefer, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can a community encourage young people to take part in local events? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • promotion on social media
  • certificates or rewards for taking part
  • involving schools and colleges
  • letting young people plan the events themselves
  • free food and activities

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do young people today have more opportunities than their parents had?
  • Is social media a good way to bring people together?
  • What responsibilities do adults have towards the next generation?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
