CAE 2026 - Test 15

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a seminar on the future of the high street. You have made notes on three ideas: lower rents for small shops, pedestrian-only streets, events and markets. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the ideas. Say which is likely to be more effective and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Proposal: your college wants to improve its social spaces; write a proposal. (b) Review: write a review of a festival or concert you have attended. (c) Article: a magazine wants articles on traditions worth keeping; write your article. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What do you enjoy about your neighbourhood?
  • What kind of music do you listen to?
  • How do you celebrate special occasions?
  • What are your plans for next weekend?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say why people might choose to get their news in these ways. Then answer: which way do you think is more reliable, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can people be encouraged to use less energy at home? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • smart meters
  • grants for insulation
  • higher energy prices
  • public information campaigns
  • energy-saving competitions

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Is it realistic to expect people to give up their cars?
  • Who has more responsibility for the environment: governments or companies?
  • Will new technology solve climate change?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
