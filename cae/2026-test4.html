CAE 2026 - Test 4

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a debate on the role of tourism in your country. You have made notes on three issues: economic benefits, damage to the environment, preserving local culture. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the issues. Say which is more significant and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Letter: write to a newspaper editor responding to an article which claims that young people spend too much time online. (b) Proposal: your town has a grant for one new facility; write a proposal recommending what to build. (c) Review: write a review of a restaurant, cafe or cultural event you have recently visited. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about the place where you grew up.
  • What did you enjoy doing as a child?
  • What are you particularly good at?
  • How do you keep fit and healthy?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of doing these jobs might be. Then answer: which job would you find more satisfying, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

What can be done to make cities more enjoyable places to live? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would make the biggest difference.

  • parks and gardens
  • cultural events
  • safer streets
  • affordable housing
  • better public transport

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Is it better to live in a city or in the countryside?
  • Should people stay in the same job for life?
  • How important is it to have a good balance between work and leisure?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
