CAE 2026 - Test 3

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a seminar on how to deal with the pressure of modern life. You have made notes on three ideas: spending more time outdoors, limiting the use of smartphones, building closer friendships. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the ideas. Say which is more effective and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Article: an international magazine is running a series on unusual jobs; write an article about a job you would like to do and why. (b) Letter: you have seen an advertisement for a volunteering placement abroad; write a letter of application. (c) Report: your school library is to be redesigned; write a report recommending changes. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What kind of music do you enjoy?
  • Tell us about a person who has influenced you.
  • What do you find most challenging about learning English?
  • How important is it to you to work as part of a team?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say why people might choose to eat in these different ways. Then answer: which way of eating do you think is more enjoyable, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

What could be done to encourage people to read more? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • free public libraries
  • book clubs
  • reading time at work
  • cheaper books
  • school reading challenges

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Does technology make people lonelier?
  • Is it important to preserve traditional skills?
  • How far should governments support the arts?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
