CAE 2026 - Test 5

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on how young people can prepare for an unpredictable future. You have made notes on three areas: learning practical skills, studying a wide range of subjects, gaining work experience. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the areas. Say which is more important and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Article: a magazine is running a series on people who have inspired others; write an article about someone who has inspired you. (b) Report: your college canteen wants to improve its service; write a report recommending changes. (c) Email: a friend asks you for advice on learning a new language; write a reply. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • How do you usually travel to work or school?
  • What do you like about the area where you live?
  • Tell us about a celebration you attended recently.
  • What are you looking forward to this year?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the pleasures and drawbacks of shopping in these ways might be. Then answer: which way of shopping do you prefer, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

What could be done to help people use less plastic? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • charging for plastic bags
  • refill stations in shops
  • better recycling
  • banning single-use items
  • education campaigns

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Is it fair to expect individuals to change their habits?
  • Should companies be forced to reduce packaging?
  • Does what consumers buy really influence big business?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
