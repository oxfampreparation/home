CAE 2026 - Test 6

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a talk on how communities can become more welcoming to newcomers. You have made notes on three ideas: language classes, social events, shared public spaces. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the ideas. Say which is more effective and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Proposal: your workplace plans a staff social event; write a proposal for the event. (b) Letter: write to your local council about noise from building works, and suggest solutions. (c) Review: write a review of a museum or exhibition you have visited. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What do you like about the place where you live?
  • Tell us about a hobby of yours.
  • What kind of films do you enjoy?
  • How do you usually celebrate your birthday?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say why people might choose to spend their holidays in these ways. Then answer: which kind of holiday would you prefer, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can people be encouraged to use public libraries more? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • longer opening hours
  • free events
  • cafes inside libraries
  • more digital books
  • school visits

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think printed books will disappear?
  • How important is it for children to read for pleasure?
  • Should public libraries be funded by the government?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
