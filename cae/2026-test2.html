CAE 2026 - Test 2

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on the changing nature of work. You have made notes on three points: working from home, automation and robots, the need to keep learning new skills. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the points. Say which is more important and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Proposal: your council wants ideas for improving public spaces for young people; write a proposal. (b) Report: your college is considering a four-day week; write a report for the principal. (c) Review: write a review of a book or series that made you rethink an issue. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What do you enjoy most about your studies or your work?
  • Tell us about a place where you feel relaxed.
  • How do you usually spend your weekends?
  • What would you like to be doing five years from now?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of travelling to work in these ways might be. Then answer: which way would you prefer, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can cities encourage people to live more sustainably? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • better public transport
  • recycling schemes
  • more green spaces
  • energy-saving homes
  • education campaigns

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Should individuals or governments do more to protect the environment?
  • How realistic is it to expect people to change their habits?
  • Is economic growth compatible with protecting the environment?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
