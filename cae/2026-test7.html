CAE 2026 - Test 7

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a talk on how cities can cope with growing populations. You have made notes on three ideas: building upwards, improving public transport, creating more green spaces. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the ideas. Say which is more important and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Article: a travel magazine wants readers' articles about a journey that taught them something; write your article. (b) Report: your company is thinking of introducing flexible working hours; write a report for the manager. (c) Review: write a review of a podcast or radio programme you would recommend. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your family.
  • What do you enjoy doing at weekends?
  • What is your favourite season, and why?
  • What would you like to learn in the future?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of living in these homes might be. Then answer: which home would you prefer, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

What can be done to help people stay healthy? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • free sports facilities
  • healthier school meals
  • a tax on junk food
  • health education
  • more cycle paths

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do people take their health for granted?
  • Is prevention better than cure?
  • Who is responsible for public health: individuals or the state?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
