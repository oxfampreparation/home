CAE 2026 - Test 17

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on advertising and consumer behaviour. You have made notes on three points: advertising aimed at children, personalised online advertising, the use of celebrities and influencers. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the points. Say which raises the greater concern and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Proposal: your town council has an unused piece of land and wants ideas for turning it into a public space; write a proposal. (b) Review: write a review of a restaurant, cafe or market that you have visited, saying who would enjoy it. (c) Letter: you have seen an advertisement for summer staff at an international youth camp; write a letter of application explaining why you would be suitable. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about a place you go to when you want to relax.
  • How has the way you spend your free time changed in recent years?
  • What kind of neighbours do you like to have?
  • Is there a tradition in your family that you particularly enjoy?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of learning in these ways might be. Then answer: which way of learning is more memorable, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can a neighbourhood become more welcoming to newcomers? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • welcome events for new residents
  • a community website or noticeboard
  • language classes
  • a buddy scheme
  • shared community gardens

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do people feel less connected to their neighbours than in the past?
  • How important is it to learn the language of the place where you live?
  • How might technology change the way communities work?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
