CAE 2026 - Test 19

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on ways of improving public health. You have made notes on three ideas: free medical check-ups, restrictions on advertising unhealthy food, more sports facilities. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the ideas. Say which is more effective and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Report: your company wants to reduce paper use; write a report with recommendations. (b) Letter: write to a former teacher to thank them for their influence on you. (c) Review: write a review of a book, film or series set in a historical period. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your best friend.
  • What is your favourite place in your town?
  • How do you prefer to travel?
  • What would you do with a completely free day?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of staying in these places on holiday might be. Then answer: which would you choose, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can people be encouraged to recycle more? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • separate bins for different materials
  • fines for not recycling
  • rewards for recycling
  • school projects
  • clearer labels on packaging

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Is it better to repair things or to replace them?
  • Do advertisements make people buy things they do not need?
  • Should companies be responsible for recycling their products?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
