CAE 2026 - Test 20

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on how to make cities safer. You have made notes on three ideas: better street lighting, more police patrols, community watch schemes. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the ideas. Say which is more effective and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Proposal: your town wants to make better use of an empty building; write a proposal. (b) Review: write a review of a restaurant, shop or website you use. (c) Report: your language school wants to introduce online classes; write a report. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your favourite meal of the day?
  • Tell us about your journey to work or school.
  • What do you like doing in the evenings?
  • How have you changed in the last five years?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say why people might choose to celebrate a birthday in these ways. Then answer: which kind of celebration would you prefer, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can a town make life better for older people? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would make the biggest difference.

  • free buses
  • community centres
  • help at home
  • training in using the internet
  • safe places to meet

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Should older people live with their families?
  • How important is it to respect traditions?
  • Do young people show enough respect for older generations?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
