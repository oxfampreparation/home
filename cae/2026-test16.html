CAE 2026 - Test 16

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on the purpose of university education. You have made notes on three points: preparing students for employment, developing independent thinking, funding for research. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the points. Say which is more important and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Letter: your local council plans to close the town library; write a letter to the council giving your views. (b) Review: write a review of a podcast, website or app that you use regularly, saying who would benefit from it. (c) Proposal: your town's tourist office wants ideas for attracting visitors outside the main season; write a proposal. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your favourite time of year, and why?
  • Tell us about someone who makes you laugh.
  • How do you usually keep in touch with friends who live far away?
  • What is something you would like to be better at?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of spending a holiday in these ways might be. Then answer: which would you prefer, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can a city make cycling safer? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would make the biggest difference.

  • separate cycle lanes
  • better street lighting
  • free helmets for cyclists
  • lower speed limits for cars
  • awareness campaigns for drivers

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Should private cars be banned from city centres?
  • Will self-driving vehicles make roads safer?
  • How might the way we travel change over the next few decades?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
