CAE 2026 - Test 12

Reading and Use of EnglishListeningWritingSpeaking

Reading and Use of English (90 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (90 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

Your class has attended a lecture on technology and healthcare. You have made notes on three points: online appointments with doctors, fitness trackers and health apps, the security of patients' personal data. Write an essay for your tutor discussing TWO of the points. Say which will have the greater impact and why. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

(a) Report: the manager of your local leisure centre wants to attract more young people; write a report assessing the current facilities and recommending changes. (b) Review: write a review of a book, film or television series that deals with a social issue, saying whether you would recommend it. (c) Letter: a local newspaper has published an article claiming that young people spend too much time online; write a letter in response. Write 220-260 words.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (15 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 15 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What kind of food do you most enjoy cooking or eating?
  • Tell us about a teacher or a boss you have particularly respected.
  • How do you usually spend your weekends?
  • Is there a place you would like to visit again? Why?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Compare the two pictures and say what the advantages and disadvantages of eating in these ways might be. Then answer: which would you choose for a special occasion, and why?

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes + 1 minute decision)

How can a company keep its employees happy and motivated? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be most effective.

  • flexible working hours
  • training and promotion opportunities
  • social events for staff
  • higher salaries
  • a comfortable working environment

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Is job satisfaction more important than a high salary?
  • Do you think people will work fewer hours in the future?
  • How might technology change the way we work?

The pictures are simple drawings. Replace them with photographs you have the right to use.
