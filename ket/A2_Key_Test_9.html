Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more.

Your friend Mia wants to volunteer at the animal centre. Write an email. Tell Mia when to go, what clothes to wear, and which animal you would like to help.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more. Use the pictures to tell a story about a lost puppy finding a home.

0 words

Listening (about 30 minutes)

These are original B1 practice scripts read aloud by your browser’s text-to-speech voice. Each recording can be played twice. This is not official Cambridge audio.

Speaking (8–10 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

  • Do you have a pet?
  • What animals do you like?
  • Have you ever helped look after an animal?
  • What do you enjoy doing at weekends?
  • Would you like to volunteer somewhere?

Part 2 — Discussion

Talk together about jobs at an animal centre. Which would you like to do? Give reasons and ask your partner.

  • feed the animals
  • walk a dog
  • clean cages
  • welcome visitors
  • brush the cats
