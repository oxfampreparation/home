Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more.

Your friend Sam wants to join the photography club. Write an email. Tell Sam when the club meets, what to bring, and what you like photographing.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more. Tell a story about a student who finds an old camera, takes a photo, and shares it with a friend.

0 words

Listening (about 30 minutes)

These are original B1 practice scripts read aloud by your browser’s text-to-speech voice. Each recording can be played twice. This is not official Cambridge audio.

Speaking (8–10 minutes per pair)

Part 1 — Interview (3–4 minutes)

  • What do you enjoy doing after school?
  • Do you like taking photos?
  • Where do you usually go with your friends?
  • What is your favourite place in your town?
  • What would you like to learn?

Part 2 — Discussion (5–6 minutes)

Talk with your partner about pictures you could take for a school display. Which would you like to photograph? Say why and ask your partner’s opinion.

Ideas: a local building, a park, a family, a pet, a favourite meal.
