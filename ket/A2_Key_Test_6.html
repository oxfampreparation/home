Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more.

Your friend Erin wants to join your cycle group. Write an email. Tell Erin when you ride, where you meet, and what to bring.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more about a child who learns to ride a bicycle.

0 words

Listening (about 30 minutes)

These are original B1 practice scripts read aloud by your browser’s text-to-speech voice. Each recording can be played twice. This is not official Cambridge audio.

Speaking (8–10 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

  • Do you ride a bicycle?
  • Where do you like going with friends?
  • What sports do you enjoy watching?
  • How do you travel to school?
  • What would you like to learn this year?

Part 2 — Discussion

Talk together about activities at a family cycling day. Which would you like to try? Give reasons and ask your partner.

  • a short bike ride
  • a bicycle safety game
  • a repair lesson
  • a picnic
  • a cycling race
