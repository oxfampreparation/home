Reading and Writing (60 minutes)

Questions 1–30 carry one mark each. Questions 31 and 32 are writing tasks.

Writing Part 6 — Email (Question 31)

Write 25 words or more.

Your friend Jamie wants to visit the planetarium. Write an email. Tell Jamie when to meet, what to bring, and what you want to see.
0 words

Writing Part 7 — Picture story (Question 32)

Write 35 words or more about friends watching the night sky.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

These are original B1 practice scripts read aloud by your browser’s text-to-speech voice. Each recording can be played twice. This is not official Cambridge audio.

Speaking (8–10 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

  • Do you like looking at the sky?
  • What subjects do you enjoy at school?
  • Where do you like going with friends?
  • What do you usually do in the evening?
  • Would you like to visit a planetarium?

Part 2 — Discussion

Discuss activities for a school astronomy evening. Which would you like to try? Give reasons and ask your partner.

  • look through a telescope
  • draw the moon
  • watch a space film
  • make a star map
  • listen to a short talk
