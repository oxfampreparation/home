B1 Preliminary (PET) - Test 1

Reading and Writing Listening Speaking

Reading and Writing (1 hour 30 minutes)

Writing Part 2 - Short Message

Write 35-45 words on your answer sheet.

You have spent the weekend staying with some English friends.

Write a card to them. In your card, you should:
• thank your friends for the weekend
• say what you enjoyed most about the weekend
• invite them to stay with you.
0 words

Writing Part 3 - Longer Piece

Write an answer to one of the questions (7 or 8) in this part. Write your answer in about 100 words.

Question 7
This is part of a letter you receive from an English friend.
"I've just seen a brilliant programme about dolphins on television. Which programmes have you enjoyed recently? How much television do you watch?"
Now write a letter, answering your penfriend's questions.

Question 8
Your English teacher has asked you to write a story.
This is the title for your story:
Walking in the rain

0 words

Listening (about 35 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Speaking (10-12 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test involves two candidates and two examiners.

Part 1 - Interview (2-3 minutes)

  • What is your name? How do you spell your family name?
  • Where do you live / come from?
  • Do you work or are you a student? What do you do / study?
  • Do you enjoy studying English? Why (not)?
  • What did you do yesterday evening / last weekend?

Part 2 - Collaborative Task (2-3 minutes)

I'm going to describe a situation to you.
A group of friends is planning a surprise party for a classmate. Talk together about the different things they will need to prepare for the party, and then decide which two things are the most important.

Preparing for a Party

Part 3 - Extended Turn (3 minutes)

Now, I'd like each of you to talk on your own about something. I'm going to give each of you a photograph of people travelling.

Candidate A: Please tell us what you can see in your photograph.

Candidate B: Now, here is your photograph. It also shows someone travelling. Please tell us what you can see in the photograph.

Part 4 - General Conversation (3 minutes)

Your photographs showed people travelling. Now, I'd like you to talk together about your own travel experiences and preferences.

  • Talk about how you usually travel to school or work.
  • Talk about the longest journey you have ever been on.
  • Discuss which forms of transport you think are the best for the environment.
  • Discuss where you would like to travel in the future and how you would get there.
