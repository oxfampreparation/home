IELTS Academic Practice Test 2

Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

ReadingListeningWritingSpeaking

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The line graph shows average daily household water use per resident in three districts between 2015 and 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people believe that university students should study a range of subjects outside their main field. Others think they should concentrate only on their chosen subject. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

What kind of place do you live in? What do you enjoy doing at home? Do you prefer working in the morning or evening? How often do you cook? What kinds of public places do you like visiting?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe a public place that you enjoy visiting.
Say where it is, when you go there, what you do there, and explain why you enjoy spending time there.

Part 3 — Discussion

What makes a public place welcoming? How can cities make public spaces useful to different age groups? Should more money be spent on parks or indoor community facilities? How have public spaces changed in recent years? Who should be responsible for maintaining them?
