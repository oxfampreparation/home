IELTS Academic Practice Test 5

Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

ReadingListeningWritingSpeaking

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The line graph shows the percentage of adults who used online public services in three regions between 2015 and 2024. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people prefer flexible working hours, while others believe employees should follow a fixed schedule. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

What kind of work would you like to do in the future? Do you prefer to work at the same time every day? How do you organise your schedule? What do you do when you need to concentrate? Do you use online services often?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe a person whose job you find interesting.
Say who the person is, what their job involves, how you know about it, and explain why you find it interesting.

Part 3 — Discussion

How do people choose a career? Should schools give students more information about different jobs? How might technology change working hours? Is job satisfaction more important than salary? What responsibilities do employers have towards workers?
