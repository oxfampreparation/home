IELTS Academic Practice Test 9

Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

ReadingListeningWritingSpeaking

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The line graph shows the annual number of participants, in thousands, on guided architecture walks in three city districts between 2015 and 2024. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people believe that old buildings should be preserved even when they are difficult to use, while others think they should be replaced with modern buildings. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

Do you like taking photographs? What do you usually take pictures of? Do you keep photographs on your phone or print them? Do you prefer looking at old or recent photographs? How do you share pictures with friends?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe a photograph that is important to you.
Say what is in the photograph, when it was taken, who took it, and explain why it is important to you.

Part 3 — Discussion

Why do people take photographs? How have phones changed photography? Should children learn how to record events accurately? Can a photograph give a complete account of an event? What rules should apply when people photograph others in public?
