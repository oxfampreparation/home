IELTS Academic Practice Test 8

Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

ReadingListeningWritingSpeaking

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The line graph shows estimated temperature changes at three research sites from 1920 to 2000, compared with a baseline period. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people believe that personal finance should be taught as a separate school subject, while others think families should teach it at home. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

Do you enjoy working with other people? What kinds of tasks do you usually do in a group? Do you prefer to lead or support a team? How do you make sure you remember important tasks? What do you do when a group disagrees?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe a time when you worked as part of a team.
Say what the team was doing, who was involved, what your role was, and explain what you learned from the experience.

Part 3 — Discussion

What makes a team work well? Should schools give students more group projects? How can a leader handle disagreement? Is competition useful in a team? What kinds of work are best done individually?
