Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The chart shows the percentage of commuters using four types of transport in Northbridge in 2010 and 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people believe that cities should make public transport free for everyone. Others think passengers should pay the full cost of their journeys. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

Where do you live? Do you work or study? How often do you use public transport? What food do you enjoy? How do you spend your free time?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe a skill that you learned from another person.
Say what the skill is, who taught you, how you learned it, and why it is useful.

Part 3 — Discussion

Should practical skills be taught at school? How has technology changed learning? Is it better to learn alone or with a teacher? Why do adults continue learning? Should employers train their staff?
