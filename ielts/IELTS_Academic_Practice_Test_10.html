IELTS Academic Practice Test 10

Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

ReadingListeningWritingSpeaking

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The line graph shows the percentage of households with rainwater tanks in three towns between 2012 and 2024. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people believe that every adult should receive basic first-aid training. Others think this should be optional. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

Do you usually make plans in advance? What do you do when plans change? Do you prefer spending free time indoors or outdoors? How do you decide what to do at the weekend? Have you ever helped organise an event?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe a time when you had to change your plans unexpectedly.
Say what you had planned, why the plan changed, what you did instead, and explain how you felt about the change.

Part 3 — Discussion

Why do plans sometimes need to change? Are people today more willing to change plans than in the past? How can organisations prepare for unexpected problems? Do people make better decisions alone or in groups? Should schools teach students how to respond to setbacks? What are the advantages and disadvantages of planning far ahead?
