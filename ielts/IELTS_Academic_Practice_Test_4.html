IELTS Academic Practice Test 4

Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

ReadingListeningWritingSpeaking

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The line graph shows annual attendance, in thousands, at three community theatre venues from 2015 to 2024. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people believe that scientific research funded by the public should be freely available to everyone. Others think researchers should be able to restrict access or charge for it. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

What object do you use every day? Do you prefer to buy new things or keep using older ones? How do you usually spend an evening? Are there any performances or events you enjoy watching? Do you like visiting new places?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe an object that is useful to you.
Say what it is, how long you have had it, how you use it, and explain why it is important to you.

Part 3 — Discussion

Why do people become attached to certain objects? How has product design changed in recent years? Should companies make it easier to repair products? Do people buy too many things today? How can governments encourage responsible consumption?
