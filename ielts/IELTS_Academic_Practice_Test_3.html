IELTS Academic Practice Test 3

Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

ReadingListeningWritingSpeaking

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The line graph shows the annual electricity generated by three tidal-energy pilot sites between 2018 and 2024. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people believe that governments should spend public money on art projects, while others think this money should be used for essential services. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

Do you enjoy helping other people? What kinds of tasks do you do at home? Do you prefer to plan your week or decide things as you go? How often do you visit a busy place? What do you usually do when you have to wait?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe a time when you helped someone solve a practical problem.
Say who the person was, what the problem was, what you did to help, and explain how you felt afterwards.

Part 3 — Discussion

Why do people help neighbours they do not know well? Should schools teach practical skills for helping others? How can towns support people who live alone? Is it better to solve problems individually or as a group? What motivates people to volunteer?
