IELTS Academic Practice Test 6

Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

ReadingListeningWritingSpeaking

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The line graph shows the percentage of road bridges fitted with monitoring sensors in three regions between 2015 and 2024. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people believe that students should be assessed mainly through examinations, while others prefer coursework and projects. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

Do you find it easy to make decisions? Do you usually ask other people for advice? What kinds of decisions do you make every day? Do you like making plans? What do you do when you change your mind?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe an important decision you made.
Say what the decision was, when you made it, who helped you, and explain why it was important.

Part 3 — Discussion

Why do some people find decisions difficult? Should young people make important decisions independently? How can people judge whether advice is reliable? Do people make better decisions with more information? Can a wrong decision sometimes lead to a positive result?
