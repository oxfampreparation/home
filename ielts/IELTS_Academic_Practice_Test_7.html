IELTS Academic Practice Test 7

Original IELTS Academic practice paper. Browser TTS is used for the listening audio.

ReadingListeningWritingSpeaking

Academic Reading (60 minutes)

Three original passages; 40 questions.

Listening (about 30 minutes)

Four parts, 40 questions. Browser TTS reads these original practice scripts. IELTS recordings in the exam are played once.

Academic Writing (60 minutes)

Task 1

Spend about 20 minutes. Write at least 150 words.

The line graph shows average crack width in three concrete samples over the 24 weeks after damage. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
0 words

Task 2

Spend about 40 minutes. Write at least 250 words.

Some people believe that schools should give more time to practical experiments, while others think students should spend more time learning theory. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
0 words

Speaking (11–14 minutes)

Part 1 — Interview

What small change has improved your daily routine? Do you prefer to plan ahead or act spontaneously? What do you do to relax after a busy day? Do you enjoy learning how things work? How often do you try a new activity?

Part 2 — Long turn

Describe a small change that improved your daily life.
Say what the change was, when you made it, why you made it, and explain how it affected your routine.

Part 3 — Discussion

Why can small habits be difficult to change? Should schools teach students how to make practical changes? How do people decide whether a new technology is useful? Is it better to improve an existing product or invent a new one? How can communities encourage people to try new ideas?
