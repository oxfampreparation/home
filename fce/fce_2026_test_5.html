FCE 2026 - Test 5

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about learning foreign languages. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should all students have to learn a foreign language at school?

Notes:
Write about:
1. travelling abroad
2. benefits for the brain
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement on an English-language website: Articles wanted: The Best Way to Spend a Rainy Weekend! Tell us what you enjoy doing when the weather is bad. Do you stay at home or go out? Give us some fun ideas! The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: You recently watched a fascinating documentary series on television or online. Write a review for your college magazine. Describe what the series was about, explain what you found interesting, and say whether you would recommend it to other students.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Chris. "I'm finally moving to your country for a year to study! What should I know before I arrive? Can you give me some advice on what clothes to bring and how to make friends easily?" Write an email responding to Chris.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us a bit about your daily routine. What time do you usually get up?
  • What is your favourite hobby at the moment? Why do you enjoy it?
  • Do you prefer going on holidays to the mountains or the beach? Why?
  • What do you think is the best way to keep in touch with friends?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to eat in these different places.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what the people might be enjoying about taking photographs in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Eating out

Candidate B's pictures: Taking photographs

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a town encourage its teenagers to do more physical exercise? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • providing free local sports clubs
  • improving school sports facilities
  • organising weekend competitions
  • building more cycle paths
  • offering discounts at the local gym

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Why do you think some teenagers stop playing sports when they get older?
  • Do you think schools should spend more time on physical education? Why?
  • Some people say that playing team sports is better than doing individual sports. Do you agree?
