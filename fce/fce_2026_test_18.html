FCE 2026 - Test 18

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about teenagers' free time. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it better for teenagers to spend their free time reading books or playing video games?

Notes:
Write about:
1. mental stimulation
2. socialising with friends
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine: Articles wanted: The Best Way to Keep Fit! Tell us about your favourite way to exercise. How often do you do it, and why would you recommend it to others? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: You recently watched a comedy television show that made you laugh out loud. Write a review for an English-language entertainment website. Describe the main characters, explain what makes the show so funny, and say whether you would recommend it to people your age.

(c) Email: You have received an email from your English friend, Sam. "I'm visiting your city next month for a huge outdoor music festival! I've never been to a festival before. What kind of clothes should I pack? Do you have any advice for staying safe in a big crowd?" Write an email responding to Sam.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your hometown. What is the most famous landmark there?
  • How often do you go shopping for clothes? Who do you go with?
  • What do you hope to be doing this time next year?
  • Do you enjoy reading about news and current events? Why or why not?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people are enjoying cooking in these different situations.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what the people might be finding difficult about travelling in these different types of weather.

Candidate A's pictures: Cooking

Candidate B's pictures: Travelling in different weather

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a town encourage its residents to reduce the amount of plastic waste they produce? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • banning all plastic bags in supermarkets
  • providing cheaper reusable coffee cups
  • running educational campaigns in schools
  • placing more recycling bins in the streets
  • charging extra money for products packed in plastic

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think it is primarily the responsibility of businesses or individuals to protect the environment? Why?
  • Some people say that modern life creates too much unnecessary waste. Do you agree?
  • How do you think shopping habits will change in the future to protect the planet?
