FCE 2026 - Test 1

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about protecting the environment. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: What can people do to help protect the environment in their daily lives?

Notes:
Write about:
1. recycling
2. using less energy
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Review: You recently ate at a new restaurant in your town. Write a review for a local English-language magazine. Describe the atmosphere and the food, and say whether you would recommend it to people your age.

(b) Article: You see this announcement on an English-language website: Articles wanted: The Importance of Hobbies. Tell us about a hobby you enjoy. How did you start doing it, and why is it important to you? The best articles will be posted on our website.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Tom. "I'm planning to visit your country next month for a short holiday. What are the best places to visit? I only have four days, so I can't travel too far. What kind of clothes should I pack?" Write an email responding to Tom.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Where are you from? What do you like about living there?
  • What do you usually do at the weekends?
  • Do you prefer to study on your own or with other people? Why?
  • Tell us about a television programme you have watched recently.

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to learn in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say how you think the people might be feeling while relaxing.

Candidate A's pictures: Learning in different ways

Candidate B's pictures: Relaxing

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a town attract more tourists? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO ideas would be the most successful.

  • organising a music festival
  • improving public transport
  • building a new shopping centre
  • providing cheaper hotels
  • creating more parks and green spaces

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think tourism brings more advantages or disadvantages to a town?
  • Why do you think some people prefer travelling to foreign countries rather than exploring their own?
  • How might the way people travel for holidays change in the future?
