FCE 2026 - Test 10

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about where people get their news. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it better to read the news online or to watch it on television?

Notes:
Write about:
1. convenience and speed
2. visual impact and emotion
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an international magazine for teenagers: Articles wanted: A Great Weekend Away! Tell us about a memorable weekend trip you have taken recently. Where did you go? What made the weekend so special? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: Your teacher has asked you to write a review of a website or a smartphone app that helps people learn a new skill. Describe what the website or app does, explain what you like and dislike about it, and say whether you would recommend it to other students.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Sam. "I'm so nervous! I've got my first ever job interview next week for a summer job at a local sports centre. What kind of questions do you think they'll ask me? Do you have any tips on how to make a good impression?" Write an email responding to Sam.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What do you usually do to relax in the evenings?
  • Tell us about a sport or physical activity you enjoy.
  • Do you prefer to spend money on things or on experiences? Why?
  • What are your main ambitions for the next five years?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about learning in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to relax in these different environments.

Candidate A's pictures: Learning

Candidate B's pictures: Relaxing

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could a local council improve the transport system in your area? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most beneficial.

  • introducing cheaper bus tickets
  • building more cycle lanes
  • providing electric city bikes for hire
  • creating car-sharing schemes
  • running buses more frequently at night

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think cars should be completely banned from city centres? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that public transport should be free for everyone. Do you agree?
  • How do you think people will travel to work in fifty years' time?
