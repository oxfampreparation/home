FCE 2026 - Test 13

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about business and young people. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should teenagers be encouraged to start their own small businesses while still at school?

Notes:
Write about:
1. learning to manage money
2. missing out on free time
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine: Articles wanted: A Great Day Out! Tell us about a wonderful day trip you took recently. Where did you go, who with, and what made the day so memorable? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: You recently read a science-fiction book or watched a science-fiction movie. Write a review for an English-language entertainment website. Describe the plot, explain what you liked or disliked about the futuristic elements, and say if you would recommend it to others.

(c) Email: You have received an email from your English friend, Sam. "I'm planning to visit your town next month during the big cultural festival! Can you tell me what kind of events usually happen? Also, what is the best way to get around the town during the busy festival weekend?" Write an email responding to Sam.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about the town or city where you grew up.
  • What do you usually do in your free time when the weather is good?
  • What kind of career or studies do you hope to pursue in the future?
  • How important is it for you to keep up to date with the news?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to help others in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about shopping in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Helping others

Candidate B's pictures: Shopping

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could the local council improve the community centre to attract more people? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • organising free fitness classes
  • upgrading the computer and internet facilities
  • opening a cheap cafe inside the building
  • hosting regular live music events
  • providing after-school clubs for children

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think people in modern cities feel less connected to their local community than in the past? Why?
  • Should young people be encouraged to do more volunteer work in their local area?
  • How important is it for a town to have public spaces where people can meet for free?
