FCE 2026 - Test 19

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about education and school rules. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should all students be required to wear a school uniform?

Notes:
Write about:
1. equality among students
2. personal expression and identity
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement on a travel website: Articles wanted: The Best Place to Hang Out! Where is the best place in your town or city for young people to spend their free time? What makes it so special? The best articles will be featured on our homepage next month.

(b) Review: You recently read a science fiction or fantasy book that you couldn't put down. Write a review for your college magazine. Describe the plot, explain what you liked about the characters or the world they live in, and say whether you would recommend it to others.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Sam. "I really want to learn how to play a musical instrument, but I don't know where to start! You're so good at playing music. Which instrument do you think is best for a beginner? How often should I practice?" Write an email responding to Sam.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about the home you live in. What do you like most about it?
  • What do you usually do in your free time during the week?
  • What are your plans for the upcoming weekend?
  • Do you enjoy travelling to new places? Why or why not?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to take photographs in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what the people might be enjoying about exercising in these different environments.

Candidate A's pictures: Taking photographs

Candidate B's pictures: Exercising

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a town be made more appealing for young people to live in? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • building a large multi-screen cinema
  • opening a modern indoor sports centre
  • running late-night public transport services
  • providing free Wi-Fi in the town centre
  • organising regular outdoor music events

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think it is important for a town to have good entertainment facilities, or are parks and green spaces more important? Why?
  • Some people say that teenagers spend too much time indoors these days. Do you agree?
  • How do you think entertainment and free time activities will change in the future?
