FCE 2026 - Test 4

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about education. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it better for students to learn practical skills or academic subjects at school?

Notes:
Write about:
1. finding a job
2. everyday life
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement on an English-language website: Articles wanted: The Best Advice! What is the best piece of advice anyone has ever given you? Who gave it to you, and how has it changed your life? The best articles will be published next week.

(b) Review: You have recently used a smartphone app or a website that helps people learn something new (for example, a language, a musical instrument, or cooking). Write a review for your college magazine. Describe how it works, what you liked or disliked about it, and say whether you would recommend it to other students.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Charlie. "I'm coming to your town for a weekend break next month! However, I don't have much money to spend. What free or cheap things can I do while I'm there? Where is a good, inexpensive place to eat?" Write an email responding to Charlie.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Who is your oldest friend? Tell us a bit about him or her.
  • What is your favourite day of the week? Why?
  • Do you prefer watching films at home or at the cinema?
  • If you could travel anywhere in the world, where would you go?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to study or work in these different environments.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what the people might be enjoying about travelling in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Studying and working

Candidate B's pictures: Travelling

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

What are the best ways for people to stay healthy in today's busy world? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO are the most effective.

  • eating a balanced diet
  • joining a local gym
  • cycling or walking to work
  • getting eight hours of sleep
  • reducing screen time

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think people worry too much about their health these days? Why?
  • Some people say it is entirely the government's responsibility to make sure citizens are healthy. Do you agree?
  • Why do you think fast food is still so popular despite the health risks?
