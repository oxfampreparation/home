FCE 2026 - Test 15

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about independence and travel. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should teenagers be encouraged to travel abroad without their parents?

Notes:
Write about:
1. learning to be independent
2. safety and potential risks
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine for young people: Articles wanted: The Best Way to Keep Fit! Tell us about your favourite physical activity. How often do you do it, and why would you recommend it to other teenagers? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: You recently visited a local museum or historical site. Write a review for an English-language travel website. Describe the exhibits or the site, explain what you found most interesting, and say whether you would recommend it to tourists.

(c) Email: You have received an email from your English friend, Anna. "I'm moving to your country next month to start university! I'm really excited but a bit nervous. What is the best way for a new student to make friends there? Is there anything special I should know about the culture?" Write an email responding to Anna.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your hometown. What is the most interesting thing to see there?
  • Do you enjoy cooking? Why or why not?
  • What are your main plans for the next few years?
  • How often do you watch films, and what type of films do you prefer?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to eat in these different situations.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what the people might be enjoying about playing these different sports.

Candidate A's pictures: Eating in different situations

Candidate B's pictures: Playing sports

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a school help its students deal with stress during exam periods? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • organising daily meditation or yoga classes
  • giving less homework in other subjects
  • providing healthy snacks and drinks for free
  • setting up a student advice and support group
  • making the library available 24 hours a day

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think life is generally more stressful for teenagers now than it was for their parents? Why?
  • Some people say that a little bit of stress is actually good for you. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for people to have a hobby that is completely disconnected from their work or studies?
