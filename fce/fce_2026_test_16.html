FCE 2026 - Test 16

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about studying and learning methods. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it better to study independently or to study together in a group with other students?

Notes:
Write about:
1. concentration and focus
2. sharing ideas and understanding
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement on an English-language educational website: Articles wanted: The Most Important Invention! What do you consider to be the most important invention in history? How has it changed the way we live today? The best articles will be published on our homepage.

(b) Review: You recently read a book that left a strong impression on you. Write a review for an English-language magazine. Describe the story or topic, explain what you liked or disliked about it, and say whether you would recommend it to others.

(c) Email: You have received an email from your English friend, Anna. "I'm going to start learning your language next month! I'm really excited but a bit nervous because I know it's difficult. What are the best ways to practice speaking and listening? Do you have any tips for a beginner?" Write an email responding to Anna.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your hometown. What is the most interesting part of it?
  • What are your main hobbies or interests outside of school or work?
  • What are your plans for the next few years regarding your studies or career?
  • Do you enjoy trying new types of food? Why or why not?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about watching sports in these different situations.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to live in these different environments.

Candidate A's pictures: Watching sports

Candidate B's pictures: Living environments

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a local library be improved to attract more teenagers? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most successful.

  • providing free, high-speed Wi-Fi
  • opening a cafe with snacks and drinks
  • creating a dedicated gaming or media room
  • hosting regular talks by popular authors
  • updating the furniture to be more comfortable

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think public libraries will still exist in fifty years' time? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that reading information on the internet is better than reading books. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for people to have a quiet place to go to in a busy town or city?
