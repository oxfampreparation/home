FCE 2026 - Test 4

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

Each recording is played twice. Use headphones and read the questions before you listen.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been discussing employment trends and workplace environments. Your teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is remote work better than working in an office?

Notes:
Write about:
1. work-life balance and commuting
2. collaboration and workplace relationships
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement on a travel website: My Favorite Urban Sanctuary! Write an article about your favorite city park. Describe what makes it special, what activities people can do there, and why urban green spaces are vital for city dwellers.

(b) Email: Your English-speaking friend Sam is planning to buy their first used car and has asked for your advice. Write an email to Sam suggesting what key things to inspect, how to manage the budget, and why taking a test drive is essential.

(c) Review: You recently played a new video game or watched a new movie. Write a review for an entertainment website. Describe the main plot or gameplay, evaluate the visual graphics and audio design, and state whether you would recommend it to others.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • How do you usually spend your free time during weekends?
  • What role does digital technology play in your daily study or work routine?
  • What is your favourite mode of travel when going on vacation, and why?
  • Tell me about a skill or language you would love to learn in the future.

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the pictures and say how the students might be feeling while preparing for exams in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the pictures and say what the advantages might be of choosing these different types of vehicles.

Candidate A's pictures: Studying and Preparing for Exams

Compare the pictures and say how the students might be feeling while preparing for exams in these different ways.

Candidate B's pictures: Choosing a Vehicle

Compare the pictures and say what the advantages might be of choosing these different types of vehicles.

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

What is the best way to encourage young people to read more books? Discuss the ideas below with your partner, then decide which TWO measures would be the most effective.

  • introducing graphic novels and comic books in school curricula
  • creating interactive community book clubs specifically for teenagers
  • reducing daily screen time limits on personal mobile devices
  • organizing regular author visits and creative writing workshops
  • making digital ebooks freely available through local public library apps

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think e-readers will eventually replace printed paper books completely?
  • Why do some people find it hard to concentrate on reading for long periods nowadays?
  • What role should public libraries play in modern local communities?
