FCE 2026 - Test 2

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about volunteering. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should all teenagers be required to do some unpaid volunteer work to help their local community?

Notes:
Write about:
1. learning new skills
2. having enough free time
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine: Articles wanted: The Best Inventions! What do you think is the most important invention of the last 100 years? Why is it so important? The best articles will be published in next month's issue.

(b) Review: You recently watched a movie that was based on a true story. Write a review of the movie for your college magazine. Tell readers what the story was about, whether you thought the actors were good, and if you would recommend it to others.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Alice. "I'm going to have a free weekend in your city next month! I want to try some traditional food and buy some souvenirs for my family. Where should I go? What should I buy?" Write an email responding to Alice.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your favourite part of the day? Why?
  • Tell us about a good friend of yours.
  • What do you enjoy doing when you have a lot of free time?
  • Do you use the internet much to learn new things?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to shop in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say how the people might be feeling about the weather in these situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Shopping in different ways

Candidate B's pictures: Experiencing the weather

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can people ensure they stay healthy with a modern lifestyle? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO ideas are the most important.

  • getting enough sleep
  • eating fresh food
  • doing regular sports
  • drinking plenty of water
  • taking time to relax

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think life is more stressful now than it was in the past? Why?
  • Some people say that schools should teach children more about healthy eating. Do you agree?
  • Do you think technology makes it easier or harder for people to stay fit?
