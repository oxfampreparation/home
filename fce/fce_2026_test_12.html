FCE 2026 - Test 12

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about school holidays. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should students have very long summer holidays, or should the holidays be spread more evenly throughout the year?

Notes:
Write about:
1. stress and exhaustion during the school term
2. forgetting what has been learned over a long break
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement on an English-language lifestyle website: Articles wanted: The Best Childhood Memory! Tell us about a specific event or day from your childhood that you will never forget. Why was it so special to you? The best articles will be published next week.

(b) Review: You recently downloaded a new smartphone application that helps people exercise or stay healthy. Write a review for your college magazine. Describe what the app does, explain what you like and dislike about it, and say whether you would recommend it to other students.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Charlie. "I'm coming to visit your town for a weekend break next month! I'm really looking forward to it. Where do you think I should stay? Also, what are the best local dishes that I must try while I'm there?" Write an email responding to Charlie.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us a little bit about the town or city where you live.
  • What are your main hobbies or interests at the moment?
  • What kind of job or studies do you plan to do in the future?
  • Do you prefer to spend your holidays relaxing or doing activities? Why?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about communicating in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to do these particular extreme sports.

Candidate A's pictures: Communicating

Candidate B's pictures: Extreme sports

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How could your local council improve the environment in your town? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • planting more trees and flowers
  • reducing the amount of traffic in the centre
  • providing more public recycling bins
  • creating new parks and green spaces
  • encouraging people to use public transport

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think individuals can really make a difference to global environmental problems? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that environmental protection should be the responsibility of businesses, not ordinary people. Do you agree?
  • How do you think our relationship with the natural environment will change in the next fifty years?
