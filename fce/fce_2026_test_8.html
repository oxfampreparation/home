FCE 2026 - Test 8

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about free time and lifestyle. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it better to spend your free time mostly indoors or outdoors?

Notes:
Write about:
1. physical health
2. the weather and seasons
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Review: You recently attended a music festival, concert, or live performance. Write a review for an English-language music website. Describe the atmosphere, comment on the quality of the performance, and say whether you would recommend going to a similar event in the future.

(b) Article: You see this announcement in an international magazine: Articles wanted: The Importance of Books! In a world full of screens, why do people still read books? Tell us about a book that changed your perspective and why reading is still a valuable habit today. The best articles will be published next month.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Chris. "I've finally decided to move to your city for a year to work! I am so excited, but I need to find somewhere to live. Which areas would you recommend for someone my age? How much should I expect to pay for rent?" Write an email giving Chris some advice.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What do you enjoy most about your hometown?
  • Tell us about a hobby you have recently started, or would like to start.
  • What are your plans for the next few years regarding your career or studies?
  • How important is it for you to spend time with your friends every week?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about travelling to work or school in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why the people might have chosen to spend time with animals in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Travelling

Candidate B's pictures: Spending time with animals

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a local park be improved to attract more people? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • planting more trees and flowers
  • adding a small cafe
  • building a children's playground
  • creating an outdoor sports area
  • improving the paths and lighting

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think green spaces in cities are as important as buildings and roads? Why?
  • Some people say that parks should be completely free, without any shops or cafes. Do you agree?
  • How can schools encourage young children to spend more time in nature?
