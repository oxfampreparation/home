FCE 2026 - Test 7

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about school holidays. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should school students be given homework to do during the long summer holidays?

Notes:
Write about:
1. having enough time to relax
2. forgetting what they have learned
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an international magazine: Articles wanted: The Best Birthday Ever! Tell us about the most memorable way you, or someone you know, has spent a birthday. Why was it so special? What made it different from a normal celebration? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: You recently started going to a new sports centre or gym in your town. Write a review for an English-language website. Describe the facilities available, comment on the staff and prices, and say whether you would recommend it to other people your age.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Chris. "I've got an interview for a weekend job at a busy cafe next week! I've never had a job interview before. What kind of questions do you think they will ask me? What should I wear, and how can I make a good impression?" Write an email giving Chris some advice.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about a typical day in your life. Do you have a strict routine?
  • What is the most popular type of food in your country? Do you like it?
  • Where did you go for your last holiday?
  • What do you think is the best way to travel long distances?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about reading in these different situations.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why the people might have chosen to be outside in these different types of weather.

Candidate A's pictures: Reading

Candidate B's pictures: Being outside

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a school become more environmentally friendly? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the easiest to achieve.

  • putting recycling bins in every classroom
  • growing vegetables in the school garden
  • turning off lights to save electricity
  • using tablets to use less paper
  • banning plastic bottles in the canteen

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think individuals can make a real difference to the environment, or is it mainly the government's responsibility?
  • Why do you think some people do not care about recycling?
  • Do you believe that protecting the environment will become more important to people in the future?
