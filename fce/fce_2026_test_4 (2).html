FCE 2026 - Test 4

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

Each recording is played twice. Use headphones and read the questions before you listen.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been discussing environmental policy and urban planning. Your teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should local councils require all new city buildings to have green roofs covered with plants?

Notes:
Write about:
1. reducing urban heat and pollution
2. construction costs and building maintenance
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in a lifestyle magazine: Local Celebrations! We are looking for articles about memorable cultural or seasonal festivals in your region. Write an article describing a festival you attended, explaining what took place, and why such events are important for community spirit.

(b) Email: Your English-speaking friend Sam is planning a two-week holiday to your country and has written asking for travel advice. Write an email to Sam suggesting the best destinations to visit, the ideal time of year to travel, and practical advice regarding local customs.

(c) Review: You recently listened to a newly launched educational podcast series. Write a review of the series for a student website. Describe the main subject matter, explain what makes the host engaging, and state whether you would recommend it to other learners.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your favourite daily routine, and why do you look forward to it?
  • How important is technology in your daily study or work life?
  • Tell me about a hobby or activity you enjoy doing when you want to unwind.
  • What is a foreign country or region you have never visited but would love to explore? Why?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the pictures and say how the people might be feeling in these different work environments.

Candidate B: Compare the pictures and say what the advantages might be of travelling in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: People at Work

Compare the pictures and say how the people might be feeling in these different work environments.

Candidate B's pictures: Travelling

Compare the pictures and say what the advantages might be of travelling in these different ways.

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

What are the most effective ways to encourage people to protect the environment in their daily lives? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO measures would have the greatest impact.

  • recycling household waste and avoiding single-use plastics
  • switching to renewable energy sources for home electricity
  • choosing public transportation or cycling instead of driving
  • buying locally produced organic food items
  • participating in local community clean-up projects

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think individual actions really make a difference in tackling global climate issues?
  • Should governments introduce stricter financial penalties for individuals or companies that waste energy?
  • How can schools best educate young children about environmental conservation?
