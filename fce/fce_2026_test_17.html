FCE 2026 - Test 17

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about politics and age. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should the legal voting age be lowered to 16 in all countries?

Notes:
Write about:
1. political awareness among young people
2. the impact of government decisions on the youth
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine: Articles wanted: A Great Festival! Tell us about a local or international festival you have attended. What were the highlights? Would you recommend it to tourists? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: You recently visited a newly opened cafe or restaurant in your town. Write a review for an English-language food blog. Describe the atmosphere, comment on the quality of the food and service, and say if you would recommend it to others.

(c) Email: You have received an email from your English friend, Alex. "I want to get fit, but I really hate going to the gym because it's boring. You're always so active! Do you have any advice on fun ways to exercise and stay healthy without using a gym?" Write an email responding to Alex.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your hometown. What is the best thing about living there?
  • How do you usually spend your free time on a Sunday?
  • What are your main ambitions for your future studies or career?
  • Do you enjoy travelling to new places? Why or why not?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about learning a language in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to watch movies in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Learning a language

Candidate B's pictures: Watching movies

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a city council encourage more people to use public transport? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • lowering the price of bus and train tickets
  • running services more frequently at night
  • creating dedicated bus lanes to speed up journeys
  • improving the cleanliness of stations and vehicles
  • offering free Wi-Fi on all public transport

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think cars should be completely banned from city centres? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that protecting the environment is entirely the responsibility of large companies. Do you agree?
  • How do you think transportation will change over the next fifty years?
