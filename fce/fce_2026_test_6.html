FCE 2026 - Test 6

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about teenagers and work. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it better for teenagers to get a part-time job or to focus entirely on their studies?

Notes:
Write about:
1. gaining real-world experience
2. having enough time to relax
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an international travel magazine: Articles wanted: The Best Way to Travel! Do you prefer travelling by train, plane, or car? What are the advantages of your favourite mode of transport? Give us some memorable examples from your own journeys! The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: You recently visited a new cafe in your town. Write a review for a local English-language website. Describe the atmosphere and the food, and say whether you would recommend the cafe to other people your age.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Chris. "I'm visiting your city for the first time next weekend! What are the most interesting places I should see? Are there any areas I should avoid? Also, what kind of clothes should I bring?" Write an email responding to Chris.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about your family. Do you have a large or small family?
  • What do you enjoy doing in your free time when the weather is bad?
  • What are your plans for next summer?
  • Do you think you will use English a lot in your future career?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about learning music in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why the people might have chosen to shop in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Learning music

Candidate B's pictures: Shopping

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a school improve its facilities to make student life better? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most beneficial.

  • modernising the school library
  • building a new sports hall
  • improving the food in the canteen
  • buying more advanced computers
  • creating pleasant outdoor seating areas

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think schools spend too much money on technology these days? Why?
  • How important is it for students to have a comfortable environment to relax in during breaks?
  • Some people say that a good teacher is much more important than good school facilities. Do you agree?
