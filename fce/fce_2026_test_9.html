FCE 2026 - Test 9

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about health and fitness. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should physical education (PE) be compulsory every day at secondary school?

Notes:
Write about:
1. physical health and obesity
2. academic performance and concentration
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an international magazine: Articles wanted: The Perfect Summer Holiday! What makes a summer holiday truly memorable? Do you prefer relaxing on a beach, exploring a city, or doing outdoor activities in nature? Tell us about your ideal trip. The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: You recently downloaded a smartphone application that helped you organise your studies or learn something new. Write a review for an English-language technology website. Describe what the app does, explain what you like and dislike about it, and say whether you would recommend it to other students.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Sam. "I've got an interview for a part-time job at a hotel next week! I'm really nervous because I've never had a job interview before. What kind of questions do you think they will ask me? What should I wear, and what is the best way to make a good impression?" Write an email responding to Sam.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your favourite subject at school or college, and why?
  • Tell us about a time you visited a different country or a new city.
  • Do you prefer watching movies at home or going to the cinema?
  • How important is it for you to keep up with the latest news?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about watching these performances.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to travel in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Watching performances

Candidate B's pictures: Travelling

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a school encourage its students to read more books? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • modernising the school library
  • inviting famous authors to give talks
  • starting student book clubs
  • providing digital tablets for reading
  • having thirty minutes of quiet reading time every day

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Some people say that reading fiction is a waste of time. Do you agree?
  • Do you think printed books will completely disappear in the future? Why/Why not?
  • How can parents encourage young children to develop a habit of reading?
