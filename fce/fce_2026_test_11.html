FCE 2026 - Test 11

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about health and fitness. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: What is the best way for young people to stay fit and healthy?

Notes:
Write about:
1. eating a balanced diet
2. playing regular sports
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement on an English-language website: Articles wanted: A Great Weekend! Tell us about an enjoyable weekend you spent recently. Where did you go and who were you with? Why was it so special? The best articles will be published next week.

(b) Review: You recently bought a new gadget or downloaded a new app that you find very useful. Write a review for an English-language technology blog. Describe what it does, what you like or dislike about it, and say whether you would recommend it to others.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, Charlie. "I'm going to start at a completely new school next month, and I'm quite nervous because I won't know anyone there. Have you got any advice on how to make a good impression and make new friends quickly?" Write an email giving Charlie some advice.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your favourite subject to study, and why?
  • Tell us about a sport or physical activity you enjoy.
  • Do you prefer to spend your holidays in a city or by the sea?
  • What is the most interesting book you have ever read?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about relaxing in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to eat in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Relaxing

Candidate B's pictures: Eating

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a school improve its facilities to make learning more enjoyable for students? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • modernising the school library
  • providing new computers and tablets
  • building better sports facilities
  • improving the food in the school canteen
  • creating outdoor seating and social areas

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think technology is becoming too important in education? Why/Why not?
  • Some people say that having a good teacher is more important than having modern school facilities. Do you agree?
  • How can parents help their children to enjoy learning outside of school?
