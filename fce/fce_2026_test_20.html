FCE 2026 - Test 20

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about technology and modern society. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is modern technology making people more isolated from each other?

Notes:
Write about:
1. communicating with friends and family
2. spending time in the real world vs. online
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement on a lifestyle website: Articles wanted: A Memorable Meal! Tell us about the best meal you have ever eaten. Where were you? Who cooked it, and what made the experience so special? The best articles will be published next week.

(b) Review: You recently watched a science fiction or fantasy television series. Write a review for an English-language entertainment magazine. Describe the plot, explain what you liked or disliked about the special effects, and say whether you would recommend it to others.

(c) Email: You have received an email from an English friend, David. "I'm finally moving to your country next month for a whole year! I'm really excited, but also a bit nervous. What are the biggest cultural differences I should expect? Can you give me some advice on how to settle in quickly?" Write an email responding to David.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your favourite time of the year? Why?
  • Tell us about a hobby you've had for a long time.
  • What kind of job would you like to have in the future?
  • Do you enjoy visiting museums and art galleries? Why or why not?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about listening to music in these different ways.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to work outdoors in these different situations.

Candidate A's pictures: Listening to music

Candidate B's pictures: Working outdoors

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can schools encourage students to eat a healthier diet? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • banning sugary snacks from the school canteen
  • providing free fresh fruit during break times
  • teaching cooking skills in practical classes
  • inviting nutrition experts to give talks
  • growing vegetables in a school garden

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think it is more expensive to eat healthily than to buy fast food? Why/Why not?
  • Some people argue that advertising unhealthy food to children should be banned. Do you agree?
  • How important is it for families to eat meals together around a table?
