FCE 2026 - Test 7

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

Each recording is played twice. Use headphones and read the questions before you listen.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been discussing education and future careers. Your teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Is it a good idea for young people to take a 'gap year' to travel or work before starting university?

Notes:
Write about:
1. gaining life experience
2. the cost involved
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an international magazine: A Film That Changed My Mind! Have you ever watched a movie that completely changed your perspective on a topic? Write an article telling us about the film, what it was about, and how it affected you.

(b) Email: Your English-speaking friend Jamie is moving to your country next month for work. Write an email offering advice on how to make friends, what customs to be aware of, and how to settle in quickly.

(c) Report: The director of your college has asked for ideas to improve the student cafeteria. Write a report describing the current situation with the food and seating, and recommend two changes that would benefit the students.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • What is your favourite meal of the day, and why?
  • How do you usually get to work or school?
  • Do you enjoy reading books, or do you prefer watching television?
  • What is something new you would like to try in the future?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the pictures and say how the people might be feeling in these different relationship situations.

Candidate B: Compare the pictures and say why the people might have chosen to do these activities as a group.

Candidate A's pictures: Relationships and emotions

Compare the pictures and say how the people might be feeling in these different situations.

Candidate B's pictures: Group activities

Compare the pictures and say why the people might have chosen to do these activities as a group.

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

What makes a workplace an excellent place to work? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO are the most important for employee happiness.

  • a high salary
  • friendly colleagues
  • flexible working hours
  • long holidays
  • a comfortable environment

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Why is it sometimes difficult for people to maintain a good work-life balance?
  • Do you think it is better to work as part of a team or independently? Why?
  • Some people say that having a job you love is more important than earning a lot of money. Do you agree?
