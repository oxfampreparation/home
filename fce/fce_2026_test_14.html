FCE 2026 - Test 14

Reading and Use of English Listening Writing Speaking

Reading and Use of English (75 minutes)

Listening (about 40 minutes)

The recordings are read aloud by your browser. Each recording can be played twice, as in the real exam. Use headphones.

Writing (80 minutes)

Part 1 - Essay (compulsory)

In your English class you have been talking about technology and education. Now, your English teacher has asked you to write an essay for homework.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

Topic: Should teenagers be completely banned from using smartphones while they are at school?

Notes:
Write about:
1. concentration during lessons
2. socialising with other students
3. ___________ (your own idea)

Write your essay in 140 - 190 words.

0 words

Part 2 - Choose ONE task

Write an answer to one of the questions (a - c) in this part. Write your answer in 140 - 190 words in an appropriate style.

(a) Article: You see this announcement in an English-language magazine: Articles wanted: The Best Live Performance! Tell us about a memorable concert, play, or comedy show you attended recently. Who was performing, what was the atmosphere like, and what made it so special? The best articles will be published next month.

(b) Review: You recently finished watching a fascinating television series that is popular right now. Write a review for an English-language entertainment website. Describe the plot, explain what you liked or disliked about the main characters, and say if you would recommend it to others.

(c) Email: You have received an email from your English friend, Sam. "I'm really struggling to improve my Spanish, and I know you learned English so quickly! Can you give me some advice? What are the best ways to practice speaking and listening outside of the classroom?" Write an email responding to Sam.

0 words

Sending opens your email app with your text. Very long texts are copied to the clipboard so you can paste them.

Speaking (14 minutes)

Practise with a partner or teacher. The real test lasts about 14 minutes.

Part 1 - Interview (2 minutes)

  • Tell us about a hobby or sport you enjoy doing in your free time.
  • How often do you usually use the internet for studying?
  • What do you like most about the town or city where you live?
  • What are your plans for the upcoming weekend?

Part 2 - Long turn (1 minute each)

Candidate A: Compare the two pictures and say why you think the people have chosen to do these different types of sports.

Candidate B: Compare the two pictures and say what you think the people are enjoying about working in these different ways.

Candidate A's pictures: Sports

Candidate B's pictures: Working

Part 3 - Collaborative task (3 minutes)

How can a town encourage its residents to be more environmentally friendly? Discuss the ideas below, then decide which TWO would be the most effective.

  • improving the public transport system
  • providing more public recycling bins
  • creating more safe cycle paths
  • banning plastic bags in local shops
  • organising community tree-planting days

Part 4 - Discussion (4 minutes)

  • Do you think it is primarily the government's responsibility or individuals' responsibility to protect the environment? Why?
  • Some people say that protecting the environment is too expensive for ordinary people. Do you agree?
  • How do you think the way we travel will change in the next fifty years to protect the planet?
